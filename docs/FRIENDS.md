# Message to send after Chrome Web Store publication

## For a video uploaded to HaySub (no installation)

Send the actual viewing link shown after publishing your uploaded video:

> Open this film here: [YOUR ACTUAL HAYSUB /watch/ LINK]
>
> Tap Play. Armenian subtitles are available in the player's language/subtitle
> menu. You can watch in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android without installing
> an extension or app. If text is off, select **Հայերեն**. Tell me if the video
> or fullscreen subtitles do not work on your device.

Do not send `/preview/...`, your owner password, storage URLs or API keys. Send
the stable `/watch/...` page link; it obtains expiring playback access itself.

## For subtitles on other websites (Chrome extension)

Replace `[ACTUAL CHROME WEB STORE LINK]` with the listing Google gives you.
Do not send this placeholder as a working installation link.

> Hi! You can now watch supported videos with my prepared Armenian subtitles.
>
> Install HaySub here: [ACTUAL CHROME WEB STORE LINK]
>
> 1. Click **Add to Chrome**.
> 2. Open your film and start it.
> 3. Click Chrome's extensions/puzzle icon, then **HaySub**. You can pin HaySub.
> 4. Click **Ընտրել հայերեն ենթագրերը գրադարանից**, search the film name and choose
>    the translation matching your film or episode.
> 5. Close the extension popup and watch.
>
> If HaySub cannot find the video, click **Գտնել ներկառուցված նվագարկիչները**,
> choose the film's frame (for example `youtube.com/embed/...`) and click
> **Թույլատրել այս նվագարկիչը**. Allow access, then select your subtitles.
>
> If the timing differs, adjust the offset: **+2** displays subtitles two seconds
> later; **−2** displays them two seconds earlier. A different cut/dub of the film
> may need a different subtitle file.
>
> You do not need Python, an API key, my website password or my computer running.
> Not every video player is supported. Please tell me the site and film if it
> does not work.
